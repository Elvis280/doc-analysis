The Problem

Why This Is Needed

Building RAG pipelines is hard enough. Preparing your data shouldn't be the bottleneck.

01
🔴

Dumb Chunking Kills Retrieval

Most RAG pipelines split documents by arbitrary token counts. This breaks sentences mid-thought, mixes topics in a single chunk, and destroys semantic meaning — leading to irrelevant retrieval results.

02
🟡

No Metadata, No Context

Raw text chunks lack context. Without knowing which document, section, or page a chunk came from, your LLM can't cite sources, can't filter by topic, and can't reason about relationships between concepts.

03
⚙️

Manual Prep Is Tedious

Building a structured knowledge base by hand means reading every page, tagging each concept, mapping relationships, and formatting output — hours of work that could be automated.

💡 KnowForge solves all three — automatically, in under a minute.
Built For Developers

Features

Everything you need to go from unstructured PDFs to production-ready knowledge bases.

AI-Powered Structuring

Converts unstructured PDF content into semantically meaningful knowledge units using LLMs — not dumb token splitting.

Rich Metadata

Every unit includes its source document, section, page numbers, type classification, and keyword tags.

Knowledge Relationships

Units are linked via typed relationships — part_of, depends_on, related_to, contrasts_with — enabling graph-aware retrieval.

Portable ZIP Output

Download everything in a single ZIP: Markdown knowledge files, JSON metadata, and a manifest.json for easy indexing.

Zero Data Retention

Your PDF is deleted the moment processing completes. The ZIP is deleted right after you download. Nothing is stored on the server.

Blazing Fast on Groq

Powered by Groq's ultra-low-latency inference. Process an 8-page PDF in well under a minute.

Common Questions

FAQs

What types of PDFs work best?

Text-heavy documents like research papers, textbooks, documentation, and reports work best. Scanned images or heavily visual PDFs may produce limited results.

Why is there an 8-page limit?

We process each page individually through an LLM for maximum extraction quality. The 8-page limit keeps the app fast and free while ensuring high-quality output.

Is my data stored on the server?

No. Your uploaded PDF is deleted the moment processing completes. The generated ZIP is deleted immediately after you download it. Nothing is stored permanently.

What's inside the output ZIP?

You get a knowledge/ folder with Markdown files for each unit, a metadata/ folder with JSON files containing structured metadata and relationships, and a manifest.json summarizing the entire knowledge base.

Why only 5 documents per day?

To keep the service free and available for everyone, we limit the entire application to 5 document generations per day. The counter resets at midnight.

What LLM powers this?

We use Groq's ultra-fast inference API to run open-source LLMs. The specific model can be configured by the administrator.
